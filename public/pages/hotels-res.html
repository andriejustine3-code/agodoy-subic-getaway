Hotels and Restaurants in Subic

Seorabeol Grand Leisure Hotel

The Seorabeol Grand Leisure Hotel is a casual, family-friendly 3-star property with a vibrant vibe, located right along Waterfront Road in the Subic Bay Freeport Zone. Compared to the Peninsular, this hotel places you directly on the active waterfront strip with easier access to scenic views and coastal dining.

  • Type: 3-star leisure and event hotel
  • Location: Waterfront Road, putting you steps away from the beach path, local restaurants, and seaside bars
  • Policies: Standard check-in at 2:00 PM and check-out at 12:00 PM

Subic Bay Peninsular Hotel

The Subic Bay Peninsular Hotel is a cozy 3-star property located at Bldg. 489, 490, 491 Canal Road, Subic Bay Freeport Zone, near the entrance of the free zone and Olongapo City.

  • Type: 3-star smoke-free accommodation.
  • Rooms: 34 spacious, air-conditioned non-smoking rooms (approx. 36 m²) including Double-Queen, Deluxe King, Deluxe Twin, and Deluxe Quad configurations
  • Policies: Check-in at 2:00 PM, check-out at 12:00 PM; children under 9 stay free using existing beds

Camayan Beach Resort

Unlike the city-based layouts of the Peninsular or Seorabeol, the Camayan Beach Resort shifts you completely into a tropical getaway. It is a relaxed, 4-star destination nestled inside the protected West Ilanin Forest Area. Surrounded by lush rainforest and a clean marine sanctuary, it is highly favored by families and nature lovers who want a true beach vacation rather than just a city stay.

  • Type: 4-star beachfront nature resort
  • Location: Camayan Wharf Road. It sits right at the edge of the free zone, directly adjacent to Ocean Adventure Park.
  • Policies: Check-in starts at 3:00 PM; check-out is at 12:00 PM. Outside food and drinks are strictly prohibited.
