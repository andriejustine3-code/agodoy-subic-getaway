Attractions in Subic

Zoobic Safari

Spanning a massive 25-hectare jungle expanse, it is recognized as the first and only tiger safari in the Philippines. Rather than a conventional, walk-by zoo layout, it offers a "backyard-style" adventure focused on highly intense, up-close interactions with apex predators.

Signature Attractions

  • The Tiger Safari: The park's main crown jewel. You board a heavily reinforced, fully enclosed cage-style safari jeepney that drives directly through a free-roaming tiger habitat. Tour guides use raw chicken to entice massive 400-pound Bengal tigers to leap directly onto the vehicle right above your head.
  • Croco Loco: A thrilling suspension bridge walk directly over a large pit populated by hundreds of snapping crocodiles. You can purchase chicken chunks to lower down and watch them feed from right below your feet.
  • Serpentarium & Close Encounters: Indoor viewing zones displaying exotic reptiles, snakes, and a dedicated corridor where you can view full-grown lions from a safe, close distance.

Ocean Adventure

Right next door to Camayan Beach Resort, Ocean Adventure offers a completely different kind of animal experience compared to Zoobic Safari. Recognized as Southeast Asia's first open-water marine theme park, it features a natural lagoon setting that opens directly into the ocean waters of Subic Bay, meaning the dolphins and sea lions reside in their genuine marine habitat rather than indoor glass tanks.

Signature Feature Presentations

  • Dolphin Friends: Hosted at Encounter Cove, this signature presentation features agile and intelligent bottlenose and spinner dolphins demonstrating natural water behaviors, high leaps, and speed runs.
  • Sea Lion Marine Patrol: An incredibly popular, light-hearted show at the Sea Lion Cinema where South American sea lions show off their clever skills while teaching kids and adults about recycling and ocean plastic conservation.
  • Wild World: Shifting away from the ocean, this eco-theater show highlights rescued jungle wildlife—including large pythons, raptors, and native Palawan bearcats—focusing on forest protection.

Inflatable Island

Inflatable Island Beach Club is the exact opposite of the quiet nature vibe at Camayan Beach. Located just outside the freeport zone along the Olongapo - Bugallon Road in Lower Kalaklan, it covers roughly 4,200 square meters, making it Asia's largest floating water park playground

Signature Floating Playground

  • The Obstacles: It features giant inflatable slides, climbing towers, balance bridges, water trampolines, human launchers, and swings.
  • The Unicorn Island: A highly popular, pastel-colored section of the course dedicated entirely to massive inflatable unicorns and rainbow tracks.
  • Safety Rules: Life vests are strictly mandatory for all guests on the water, and the entire course is actively monitored by a team of lifeguards. Guests must attend a brief safety orientation 30 minutes before their scheduled water time.
